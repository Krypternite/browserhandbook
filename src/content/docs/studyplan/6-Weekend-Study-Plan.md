# Weekend Study Plan

## Goal

You do not need to become a SQL expert in one weekend.

You need to demonstrate:

```text
Technical fluency
+
Analytical reasoning
+
Product thinking
+
Stakeholder judgment
+
Clear communication
```

---

# Saturday Morning — SQL Fundamentals

Focus on:

- SELECT
- WHERE
- DISTINCT
- COUNT
- SUM
- AVG
- GROUP BY
- HAVING
- ORDER BY
- LIMIT
- NULL
- COALESCE
- CASE

Do not just read.

Write queries.

---

# Saturday Afternoon — JOINs

Master:

- INNER JOIN
- LEFT JOIN
- table relationships
- primary/foreign keys
- table grain
- duplicate rows after joins

Most important question:

> What does one row represent?

Practice:

- users with orders
- users without orders
- revenue by customer
- revenue by product
- products with no orders

# Do Not Spend Your Weekend Doing This

Avoid spending hours on:

- obscure SQL syntax
- database administration
- query optimization internals
- stored procedures
- triggers
- advanced database architecture
- every possible Scrum certification detail
- memorizing frameworks

Prioritize practical analytics and product judgment.

# Tomorrow's Mock Case

Use the Toy Store dataset.

Scenario:

> "Traffic has grown significantly, but the Head of Product is concerned that revenue isn't growing proportionally. Investigate the problem and recommend what the product team should do."

During the mock interview, practice:

```text
Clarify
↓
Define metrics
↓
Understand table grain
↓
Write SQL
↓
Analyze
↓
Segment
↓
Identify driver
↓
Form hypothesis
↓
Recommend action
↓
Define success metric
```

This combines your SQL preparation with Product Owner thinking.
